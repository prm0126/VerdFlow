How a study moves through VerdFlow

From order to peer review, in one traceable path

  1. ReceiveThe hospital's Mirth sends the order. VerdFlow stores it before it acknowledges it, so nothing is lost.
  2. VetServices that need vetting wait for a radiologist to approve a protocol.
  3. DecideApproved rules pick the team and check every radiologist: shift, break, privileges, capacity.
  4. AssignThe chosen radiologist is reserved and the assignment goes out as HL7 to the reporting system.
  5. ReportAccept, decline, preliminary and final come back. A decline is routed again at once.
  6. ReviewTurnaround is tracked, and a share of final reports goes to an independent peer reviewer.

Operations

See the whole service at a glance, and every study in detail

For reporting coordinators: what needs attention now, where every study is, and exactly why each one went where it did.

Operations dashboard

Menu → Dashboard

One page for the state of the service, refreshed every 10 seconds.

  • Briefing: a short written summary that leads with what needs action now.
  • Intake: messages in the last hour, backlog and its age, quarantined messages, reused control IDs.
  • Needs attention: open and unacknowledged escalations, overdue and at-risk turnaround, vetting waiting.
  • Studies by stage, final reports in 24 hours, radiologists available now.
  • Delivery to the third party: waiting, uncertain, failed or rejected.
  • Same-patient continuity kept or broken, and why studies need a coordinator.

Worklist

Menu → Worklist

Every study, newest first, filtered by state or searched by accession.

  • Priority shown as STAT, Urgent or Routine.
  • The team and the radiologist who has it now, or who reported it.
  • Turnaround: on time, at risk or overdue, with the time it is due.
  • State in plain words: waiting for vetting, sent, accepted by radiologist, preliminary, final.
  • Patient details are masked for anyone without permission to see them.

Study page

Worklist → click a study

The full story of one study on one page.

  • Explain in plain English: a short written account of where the study is and why.
  • What came in: each HL7 message for this study and what happened to it.
  • The decision: rule-set version, the rule that chose the team, the radiologist selected.
  • Coordinator actions: Route again, Reassign (before a report exists) and Cancel, each with a reason and audited.

Why this radiologist, and why not the others

Study page, further down

Every candidate is listed with their rank, load, clock-in time and the exact reason anyone was left out, such as AT_CAPACITY, ON_BREAK, NO_SHIFT or RULE_CT_NEEDS_MSK.

  • The order used is stated: same patient first, then whoever clocked in first (or least loaded).
  • Turnaround clocks show each target, the rule version it came from, and met, late or due.
  • Assignments, what was sent with each delivery attempt, and the full history.

Past decisions stay explainable after rules change: each one records the facts and rule version it used.

Escalations

Menu → Escalations

Raised automatically, closed automatically.

  • Overdue and At risk when a turnaround target is passed or close.
  • Needs a coordinator when nobody eligible could take a study, or a room or delivery problem holds it.
  • Coordinators Acknowledge so the team can see someone has it.
  • Each closes by itself when the step is done or the study moves on, with the reason recorded.

Errors and reconciliation

Menu → Errors
  • Delivery problems: failed, rejected or uncertain sends, with "may have arrived" when the destination might already hold it. Retry resends the same message under the same control ID.
  • Quarantined messages with the reason, for example an unknown modality. Fix the master record, then Process again.
  • Control ID reused with different content: kept, never processed, until someone has investigated.
Clinical work

Vetting before routing, peer review after reporting

For radiologists: the requests waiting for a protocol, and the reviews assigned to them.

Vetting

Menu → Vetting · radiologists
  • Services marked "needs vetting" (MR brain in the demo) wait here, STAT first.
  • Approve with a protocol from the service's list: the study is routed straight away.
  • Do not approve with a reason: the workflow ends and later updates are ignored.
  • The vetting wait has its own turnaround target and escalates when overdue.

Peer review

Menu → Peer review
  • A set share of final reports is chosen for review (20% in the demo). The same study is always in or out of the sample, so retries never change it.
  • The reviewer is never the original reporter, must hold the privilege for that modality and hospital, and has a daily limit.
  • When nobody can take a review it is recorded as skipped, with the reason.
  • Only the assigned reviewer can record the outcome: agree, minor or major discrepancy.
Assistant

Ask in your own words: "What is scheduled under my name?"

A read-only assistant inside VerdFlow. It answers from VerdFlow's own data, with the person's own permissions.

Assistant

Menu → Assistant
  • Your rota: shifts, leave and breaks under your name, and whether you can take new work right now.
  • Your work: studies assigned to you with turnaround, your open peer reviews, vetting waiting.
  • The team: who is on shift. A study by accession. Turnaround performance. Open escalations.
  • Also: Explain in plain English on study pages, the dashboard Briefing, and drafting rules from a description for a person to review.

It reads but never changes anything, and never sees patient names or identifiers. Without an AI key it answers common questions from built-in templates. With Claude connected it handles free-form questions.

People and rota

Work only goes to people who are on shift, present and free

Schedules, breaks and clock-in are part of every decision, not a separate list.

Availability

Menu → Availability
  • Can take new work, or exactly why not: not on shift, on a break, no workstation heartbeat, at capacity.
  • Clocked in time: the first workstation heartbeat of the session or the radiologist's own sign-in.
  • Start or end a break on the spot; change status and capacity (work units).
  • Changing status never withdraws work a radiologist already holds.

Rota

Menu → Rota
  • A week view of shifts (reporting or on call), leave, training and breaks.
  • Plan breaks ahead, such as lunch 13:00–13:30. Nothing is allocated during a break.
  • Overlapping shifts and leave without an end date are refused. Every change is audited.

Users

Menu → Users · administrators
  • Add people with roles: administrator, coordinator, radiologist, rule author, rule approver, integration engineer, viewer.
  • Each new user gets a single-use link to set their own password, valid for 72 hours. Passwords are never emailed or shown.
  • Changing roles or suspending someone signs them out everywhere at once.
Rules and masters

Your allocation policy, written down and approved

Routing, exclusions, continuity, ranking, turnaround targets and peer review live in one versioned rule set. A different person must approve every change.

Rules

Menu → Rules
  • Routing to a team, lowest priority number first: "All CT to musculoskeletal radiologists".
  • Exclusions: "CT only for radiologists with the MSK specialty", "STAT studies go to consultants only".
  • Same patient, same doctor: by encounter, or by patient and specialty.
  • Then: the least loaded radiologist, or whoever clocked in first this shift.
  • Turnaround targets per priority, and the peer-review share and daily limit.

Draft → submit → approved by someone else → published. The previous version is retired, never overwritten.

Rule editor

Rules → New draft
  • Basic view: pick a fact, an operator and a value. No code, no formulas.
  • Advanced view: the same rule set as JSON, checked by the same validation.
  • Describe a change in plain words and Claude proposes the rules for you to check. Nothing is saved until you do.
  • Missing data never counts as a match, and unclear cases exclude rather than include.

Masters

Menu → Masters
  • Hospitals, rooms (with allowed modalities and open or closed), modalities and work units, services and vetting protocols.
  • Teams, employees, and radiologists: specialties, modality privileges, hospitals, teams, capacity.
  • Integration partners: source, reporting or presence, allowed facilities, send limits and API keys.
  • Withdrawing a privilege takes effect immediately, even for a decision being made at that moment.
HL7 integration

Every message in and out, readable field by field

For integration engineers: the full message log, and a safe way to send test messages through the real intake path.

HL7 messages: received

Menu → HL7 messages → Received
  • Every message from every partner: time, type, control ID, study, and the result (processed, ignored or quarantined with the reason).
  • View shows one segment per line; click a segment to see its numbered fields: PID-3, OBR-24.
  • Opening a message is recorded in the audit log.

HL7 messages: sent to the third party

Menu → HL7 messages → Sent to third party
  • Every assignment and cancellation, with the study, radiologist, delivery state and ACK code.
  • Each attempt is listed. A lost ACK is treated as "delivery uncertain", never as permission to send a conflicting assignment.
  • One slow destination cannot hold up the others: each has its own in-flight limit.

Send a message

Menu → HL7 messages → Send a message
  • Start from a template: new order, cancel, or a radiologist's accept, decline, preliminary or final for a study's current assignment.
  • Edit it, choose the partner, and send. You see the acknowledgment a real partner would get.
  • It goes through exactly the same checks as a message from Mirth, and every send is audited.
Access and audit

Who did what, and when

Audit log

Menu → Audit log · administrators
  • Sign-ins and failed attempts, rule changes and approvals, rota and availability changes.
  • Every raw HL7 view, manual send, reassignment, cancellation and master-data change.
  • Entries cannot be edited or deleted, by anyone.

Sign in

  • Each person sees only the menu items their role allows.
  • Accounts lock for 15 minutes after five wrong passwords, and sign-in attempts are rate limited.
  • Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies and every change needs a same-site request header.
Built in

What VerdFlow will not do

Lose an order

An order is acknowledged only after it is stored. A retried message is recognised and processed once.

Double-book a study

One active assignment per study, and capacity is checked again, under a lock, at the moment of reserving.

Ignore the rota

No shift, a break, leave, stale presence or full capacity means no new work. Each is named in the trace.

Guess on missing data

A rule that cannot be checked excludes the radiologist rather than including them.

Send conflicting HL7

A retry reuses the same control ID. A cancellation never overtakes its assignment.

Change rules quietly

A second person approves every rule set, and each decision keeps the version it used.
